## 1. Where to verify (sources)
- **SNSF** — Data Portal / P3 grant database (data.snf.ch) → grants, amounts, dates, team.
- **ERC / Horizon Europe** — CORDIS (cordis.europa.eu) → EU projects.
- **Innosuisse** — project news / press; often via the industry partner.
- **University pages** — group "projects", "team", "open positions" pages.
- **Google Scholar** — activity, co-authors, recent output (proxy for group vitality).
