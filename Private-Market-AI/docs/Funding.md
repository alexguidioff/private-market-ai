# Funding Strategy

**Author:** Alessandro Guidi — v0.1

> **Key insight:** grants require a **host group first**, then the proposal.
> Find the group → then write the grant. Never the reverse.

---

## 1. Funding ladder

| Stage | Instrument | Amount (verify) | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prerequisite | Collaboration / RA / Research Engineer | salary | University group | Unlocks everything else |
| Research → innovation | **BRIDGE Proof of Concept** | up to ~CHF 130k / 12 mo (salary + direct costs) | Researcher + host | Funds innovation based on peer-reviewed research you contributed to; periodic calls; decision ~3 mo after submission |
| Applied innovation | **Innosuisse Innovation Project** | applied research budget | Company + Swiss research partner | Needs a lab (HSG/ETH/EPFL) + industry implementation partner (VC, family office, bank, data provider) |
| Company (later) | **Innosuisse Start-up Innovation Project** | up to ~70% of direct costs (≥30% by startup) | Swiss startup | After Swiss company + MVP + validation |
| Translation | **ETH Pioneer Fellowship** | ~CHF 120k/12 mo or 180k/18 mo (Deep-Tech) | ETH host prof required | Turns ETH research into a spin-off |
| Translation | **EPFL Innogrant** | up to ~CHF 100k (founder salary, 1 yr) | Hosted in an EPFL lab | Must be incubated in an EPFL lab, clear startup vision |
| Translation | **UZH Entrepreneur Fellowship** | ~CHF 100k/12 mo (Digital Innovation); 150k/18 mo (Bio/MedTech) | Tied to UZH research | Digital Innovation covers AI systems |
| Company | **Venture Kick** | up to ~CHF 150k in 3 stages + network/coaching | Founder | Milestone-based pre-seed, science-based startups |

> ⚠️ All amounts/durations are indicative — **verify on official SNSF / Innosuisse / ETH / EPFL /
> UZH / Venture Kick pages** before relying on them.

---

## 2. Sequencing (realistic timeline)

```
Oct   host / group found
Nov–Jan  write grant proposal
Feb   submit
Spring–Summer  decision
Then  project starts
```

Three levels of engagement, by speed:
- **Level 1 — Collaboration:** ~2 weeks.
- **Level 2 — Research Assistant:** 2–6 months.
- **Level 3 — Grant:** 6–12 months.

---

## 3. What each funder wants to see

- **BRIDGE:** a credible research-to-innovation path, an academic host, and a market rationale.
- **Innosuisse:** an implementation partner (company) + research partner; clear innovation.
- **Venture Kick:** a fundable venture story and milestones.

Having "Research Advisor: Prof. X (ETH) / Prof. Y (HSG)" on the application changes perception
dramatically.
