# Academic Profile — Build Checklist

**Author:** Alessandro Guidi — v0.1

> Purpose: assemble the documents used in every email, application, and interview.
> The Academic CV is the anchor; everything else is built around it.

---

## 1. Academic CV (priority ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
Style: MIT/Stanford, clean, LaTeX, 2 pages max. **Not** the Amazon CV.
Its job: make a professor think *"this person can publish with us"* — not *"this person does PM."*

Suggested title: **Researcher in AI for Private Markets** (or *Machine Learning Researcher |
Product Manager*). Justified by an accepted publication + a research thesis.

Section order:
1. **Header** — name, MSc (ESCP), Product Manager @ Amazon, PMP, email, LinkedIn, GitHub,
   Google Scholar, ORCID. *No photo, no birth date, no address.*
2. **Research Interests** — ML for private markets, XAI, entrepreneurial finance, VC, startup
   valuation, decision support systems, AI for investment decision-making.
3. **Education** — ESCP (Master in Management, 2024–2026; digital transformation, PM, ML);
   Università di Torino (Bachelor); Amazon apprenticeship.
4. **Publications** — Guidi, A. *Can Non-Financial Signals Price Private Companies? A ML
   Approach to Startup Valuation.* Accepted, WI2026 Student Track. *(Keep near the top.)*
5. **Working Papers** — Master Thesis (ESCP, 2026).
6. **Research Experience** — thesis as a research project: PitchBook, GEM, Random Forest,
   SHAP, temporal validation.
7. **Professional Experience** — Amazon, Capgemini (short; capability-focused).
8. **Technical Skills** — Python, SQL, Git; scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, pandas, NumPy;
   Matplotlib, Plotly; Jupyter, VS Code.
9. **Awards** — WI2026 acceptance.
10. **Languages** — Italian, English, French.
11. **Research Pipeline** *(the differentiator):* AI for Startup Valuation → XAI for VC →
    LLM-based Due Diligence → AI Investment Decision Support.

**Do NOT include:** PMP at top, PowerPoint/Excel/MS Office, "soft skills", "problem solving",
"team player".

---

## 2. Research Statement (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
1 page. Title: *AI for Private Market Decision Systems*. Contents: background, research vision,
current paper, where you're going, 3 future research directions.

Add two short sections:
- **"What I'm looking for"** — research collaboration / RA / Research Engineer role; access to
  datasets & projects; a publishable research line and, later, grants (BRIDGE, Innosuisse).
- **"Future Research Portfolio"** — 5–6 paper ideas, each in a few lines (problem, method, impact).
