Dear Professor Ademi,

I am writing to enquire about the possibility of pursuing doctoral research under your supervision at
the Institute of Technology Management (ITEM-HSG).

I have followed your work on how venture capitalists actually decide — the conjoint experiments on
CVC managers' evaluation of opportunities, and your work on syndication decisions — closely, and
what struck me most is the move from "which ventures succeed" to *how investors weight information
when they choose*. Your entry on venture capital screening frames exactly the act my project wants to
formalise. My proposed work starts there but asks the normative counterpart: given a decision, which
missing piece of information — at what cost — most improves it? Not which feature predicts success,
but which diligence step is worth doing.

This builds on my paper *Can Non-Financial Signals Price Private Companies?* (accepted, WI2026
Student Track), which shows, on 3,403 deals under a strict out-of-time holdout, that non-financial
signals price private companies competitively with financial baselines, and that **investor-syndicate
structure** is the dominant driver — a finding that connects directly to your work on syndication,
and that exposes the ceiling of a purely predictive approach. I would envisage ITEM-HSG as the domain home, with an ETH
methodological co-advisor for the modelling; the project is feasible without proprietary data (a
point-in-time public reconstruction plus a virtual-fund simulator provide ground truth).

I attach a brief project outline and my CV for your consideration. I would be very grateful for the
opportunity to discuss whether there is scope for a doctoral collaboration, and whether there are
funded positions or doctoral assistantships within your group for 2026.

For the practical side: my ESCP Master in Management (Corporate Finance / Financial Markets) is
conferred in September 2026, I am available from then, and as an Italian citizen I am eligible to
work in Switzerland without a permit procedure. I would be looking to start by January or February
2027.

Thank you very much for your time.
