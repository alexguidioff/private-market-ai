Dear Professor Tykvová,

I am writing to enquire about the possibility of pursuing doctoral research under your supervision at
the Chair of Private Markets and Alternative Investments (School of Finance, HSG).

I have followed your empirical work on private markets closely — in particular your research on
venture and private-equity syndication and your survey mapping the VC/PE research agenda. What
strikes me is that the literature establishes *what* drives private-market outcomes, while the prior
question — *which* information is worth acquiring before a decision, and at what cost — remains
largely open. That is the question my project sets out to formalise: not which factor predicts
success, but which piece of diligence is worth doing.

This builds on my paper *Can Non-Financial Signals Price Private Companies?* (accepted, WI2026
Student Track), which shows, on 3,403 deals under a strict out-of-time holdout, that non-financial
signals price private companies competitively with financial baselines, and that
**investor-syndicate structure** is the dominant driver — a finding that connects directly to your
work on syndication, and that exposes the ceiling of a purely predictive approach. I would envisage
your Chair as the empirical-finance home for the project, with an ETH methodological co-advisor for
the modelling; the work is feasible without proprietary data (a point-in-time public reconstruction
plus a virtual-fund simulator provide ground truth).

I attach a brief project outline and my CV for your consideration. I would be very grateful for the
opportunity to discuss whether there is scope for a doctoral collaboration, and whether there are
funded positions or doctoral assistantships within your group for 2026.

Thank you very much for your time.
