## Why this email comes before the portal application

Three verified facts make the pre-check the correct first step:

1. **D-INFK states it invites applicants holding a Master's in computer science *or a related field*.**
   My degrees are a Master in Management (ESCP) and a BSc in Business & Management (Turin) — neither is
   CS. Whether they count as "related" is not my call to make, and guessing wastes weeks.
2. **The official procedure puts recognition first.** ETH's registration guidance instructs supervisors
   to check with the central doctoral administration whether a candidate's studies are recognised and
   whether admission is possible *before* accepting them — employment and further steps follow only
   after provisional admission is confirmed. So a professor cannot hire me until this box is ticked
   anyway.
3. **Timing.** Formalities can take up to 8 weeks (less for EU citizens, but a security screening also
   applies to doctoral applications). Against a target start before March 2027, this must move in
   September, not December.

There is also a likely-good outcome worth asking about explicitly: **extended doctoral studies**, the
route ETH uses for candidates whose prior education does not fully cover the discipline (additional
graded credit points on top of the regular requirement). If that is the path, I want to know now and
say so openly in the application rather than have it surface as an objection.

## Notes on the draft

**Why volunteer the weakness.** They will discover the degree background in the first thirty seconds
of reading a transcript. Naming it first converts it from a disqualification they find into a question
they answer, and makes the two strong signals (110/110 summa cum laude, 3.8/4.0) do their work instead
of looking like distraction.

**Why name LAS.** Recognition decisions are departmental, but stating the target group lets them route
the question and signals this is a real application rather than a speculative enquiry.

**Why ask about extended doctoral studies by name.** It shows the regulations were read, and it offers
them an easy constructive answer instead of a binary yes/no.

**Do not** attach the outline or CV to this email. It is an administrative question, not an application.
Keep it short.

## ⚠️ D-MTEC uses a DIFFERENT address for the same question

D-MTEC states that its own doctoral administration **does not handle applications or admissions**, and
routes all admissions questions to the **central** ETH Doctoral Administration:

- **D-MTEC / central recognition check:** `doktorat@ethz.ch`
- **D-INFK doctoral administration:** `doctorate@inf.ethz.ch`
- **D-INFK application portal contact:** `phd@inf.ethz.ch`

Since the underlying question — "is an ESCP Master in Management recognised for admission to an ETH
doctorate?" — is *central*, not departmental, the efficient move is to ask **both**: `doctorate@inf.ethz.ch`
for the D-INFK-specific "related field" judgement, and `doktorat@ethz.ch` for the general recognition
ruling that also unlocks D-MTEC. One question, two recipients, no duplicated work.

The third outcome is not a failure — it is the information that saves the most time, and it points at
the department where the profile is a strength rather than an exception.
