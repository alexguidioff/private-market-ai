Dear Professor He,

I am writing to enquire about the possibility of pursuing doctoral research under your supervision
in the Optimization & Decision Intelligence Group at ETH Zurich.

I have followed your group's work on optimisation and decision-making under uncertainty closely, and
what struck me most is that you treat information itself as something with a cost — your work on the
bias–variance–cost trade-off of stochastic optimisation asks how much information to acquire and when
it stops being worth it. My proposed project starts precisely there, but moves that trade-off from an
estimation setting into a *decision* one: it treats the acquisition of information in an investment
decision as a sequential, cost-constrained problem, and asks which piece of information most improves
the decision per unit cost (with your work on no-regret learning in constrained MDPs as the natural
machinery for the budget).

Concretely, I study the question prior to prediction: given an investment decision under
uncertainty, *which* missing information — at what cost — most improves it. This builds on my paper
*Can Non-Financial Signals Price Private Companies?* (accepted, WI2026 Student Track), which shows,
on 3,403 deals under a strict out-of-time holdout, that non-financial signals price private companies
competitively with financial baselines, and that investor-syndicate structure is the dominant driver
— which is what exposes the ceiling of a purely predictive approach. Your optimisation-under-uncertainty toolbox is exactly what this
question needs; I would envisage a domain co-advisor at HSG for the private-markets side.

I hold a background in machine learning applied to private markets, with an accepted publication in
the area, and the project is feasible without proprietary data (a point-in-time public
reconstruction plus a virtual-fund simulator provide ground truth). Code and manuscript are
available on request.

I attach a brief project outline and my CV for your consideration, and I am also submitting the
group's application form — I wanted to reach out directly first to check whether this direction fits
your current research priorities. I would be very grateful for the opportunity to discuss whether
there is scope for a doctoral collaboration, and whether there are funded positions or doctoral
assistantships within your group for 2026.

Thank you very much for your time.
