# Options for September 2026 onwards — Zurich and online

**Compiled 2026-07-29.** Context: ESCP degree and Amazon apprenticeship both end September 2026. The
D-INFK doctoral application is in flight but the realistic ETH doctoral intake for someone finishing in
September is a **February–March 2027 start, hired September to November 2026**. So there is a five-month
income gap even in the good scenario. These options are ranked by whether they address that.

---

## A. Paid, no doctorate required — this is the category that actually solves the problem

ETH employs **scientific assistants at grades I and II** as an official career level with a fixed salary
paid in monthly instalments ([employment and salary](https://ethz.ch/en/the-eth-zurich/working-teaching-and-research/working-conditions/employment-and-salary.html)),
and pay is adjusted alongside doctoral students and postdocs
([Staffnet](https://ethz.ch/staffnet/en/news-and-events/internal-news/archive/2024/12/pay-adjustment-for-doctoral-students-postdoctoral-researchers-and-scientific-assistants.html)).
A Master's degree is enough. **jobs.ethz.ch listed 109 open positions when checked.**

### 🔎 Side discovery worth more than any single posting

The listing includes an administrative role at an ETH **Institut für Maschinelles Lernen mit Fokus
Finanzen** (Institute for Machine Learning with a focus on Finance). The role itself is irrelevant, a 40%
office manager post. The *existence of the unit* is not: it is ETH doing precisely the ML-and-finance
intersection, and it did not appear in the D-MTEC chair screen in `DMTEC_Chair_Screen.md`. Identify the
professor and add it to that file.

**Strategic point that survives the corrections.** A paid scientific-assistant or engineer post is
probably a *more* realistic entry to ETH than the doctorate, and it is not a detour: it is paid, it is
inside ETH, it produces an internal reference, and it is the profile that converts into a doctoral position
at the next intake. ETH's own advice remains that the
[best route is to contact the professorship directly](https://ethz.ch/en/doctorate/finding-a-place.html).

**On the "management, not economics" objection.** Three of the four roles above require neither an
economics degree nor a doctorate. And the transcript understates itself: the ESCP finance track included
Empirical Methods in Finance, which is econometrics, alongside Firm Valuation and Derivatives, and the
WI2026 paper uses an out-of-time holdout placed across a regime break, which is an econometric design
decision rather than a management one.

## B. In person in Zurich — the network is what does the hiring

- **[ETH AI Center Associated Researchers Meetup](https://ai.ethz.ch/research/events/researchers-meetup.html)** —
  held around the end of each semester month, to present ongoing research, start collaborations and
  network over an apéro. Highest value per hour of anything on this page: it is the room where the people
  who post the jobs in section A actually are.
- **[Pre-NeurIPS poster session](https://ai.ethz.ch/research/events/poster-sessions/pre-neurips-2025.html)** —
  the ETH AI Center ran a Zurich edition in 2025; expect a 2026 edition around November or December. This
  is a **deadline-bearing target for EXP-005 as a poster**, which converts a private markdown file into
  something presented in person at ETH.
- **[Swiss AI Safety Days 2026, 7–8 November, ETH Zurich](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/ArN3yrLztxjXeKC88/save-the-date-swiss-ai-safety-days-2026-7-8-november-eth)** —
  successor to the 2025 one-day Zurich event, expanded to a full weekend. Relevant only if the AI-safety
  evaluation direction is worth keeping open.
- **[ETH AI Center research events](https://ai.ethz.ch/research/events.html)** generally, plus departmental
  seminars, which are normally open to walk into.
- **Apertus / Swiss AI Initiative** — ETH, EPFL and CSCS released
  [Apertus 1.5](https://ai.ethz.ch/news-and-events/ai-center-news/2026/07/apertus-15-building-the-next-generation-of-open-ai-infrastructure.html)
  in July 2026, a fully open LLM. Contributing to an open Swiss project is a public artefact with local
  visibility.

## C. Online — solves the missing public artefact, which is the other open blocker

**[Cohere Labs Open Science Community](https://cohere.com/research/open-science)** is the best fit found.
It is built for exactly the status problem: they collaborate with independent researchers, and those who
publish with their affiliation alone are recognised as **Community Researchers**, explicitly framed as an
achievement because it happens without a formal lab behind it
([details](https://labscommunity.cohere.com/get-started/community-research)). There is an
[application](https://cohere.com/research/open-science/application), and the join link is emailed within
about a week. They also run a
[community blog that accepts experiment reports and research notes](https://labscommunity.cohere.com/blog/2026/welcome-to-cohere-labs-blog/).
*Content rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.*

**Why this matters specifically:** EXP-005 is already written. This is the shortest path from a private
markdown file to a public, co-signed artefact, which is the exact thing missing from the Agentic Systems
Lab form and from any September email to a chair.

**BlueDot Impact** — assessed separately. Free AI-safety courses plus a 30-hour project sprint. Good
programme, wrong window: it consumes the August-to-November weeks when the ETH hiring wave opens, and any
artefact produced would be in AI safety rather than private markets, so it diversifies the file instead of
deepening it. Only the project sprint is worth considering, and only if the safety direction is being kept
open deliberately.
