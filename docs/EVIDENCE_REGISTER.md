## Required classifications
- `repository evidence`: a dated artifact exists locally.
- `external evidence`: independently verifiable source or observation.
- `assumption`: plausible but not observed.
- `hypothesis`: deliberately falsifiable claim.
- `unknown`: required fact not yet established.
