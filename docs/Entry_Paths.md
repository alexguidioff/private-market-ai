# Entry Paths into the Swiss Research Ecosystem

**Author:** Alessandro Guidi — v0.1
**Level:** 🧭 **[NORTH STAR — private]** — strategy note.

> **Reframing:** the goal is not to "enter a university". It is to **enter an ecosystem**.
> A PhD is only one door — and probably not the first for this profile.
> The real target is not "a PhD position" but *a professor who says: "Alessandro, let's work
> together."* The contract form comes after.

---

## The false binary
The assumption "either a PhD or a startup" is wrong. In Switzerland there is a **third path**,
better suited to a profile with Amazon + PM + ML + business + an accepted paper.

---

## Six ways in (ranked by fit)

### 1. Research Assistant / Scientific Collaborator ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Try this first. You do **not** need a PhD. Groups (esp. at ETH/HSG/EPFL) routinely hire RAs /
scientific assistants on **already-funded projects**. Positions are posted on official portals;
many groups also recruit directly. Upside: paid, data access, publications, and you can decide
about a PhD *later*.

### 2. Research Engineer ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Likely the ideal role. AI labs increasingly hire hybrid figures — strong engineers/ML engineers
who also publish, the research equivalent of a Senior Engineer. **Amazon on the CV puts you much
closer to this than to a fresh-graduate PhD applicant.**

### 3. Industry Collaboration ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
Keep an industry job and collaborate with a group in parallel (e.g. one day/week, or on a
single paper). More common than it looks.

### 4. Visiting Researcher ⭐⭐⭐☆☆
Usually unpaid, not enough on its own — but a low-friction way to start a relationship.

### 5. Grant-based Research ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
The most interesting route: find a professor → co-write a project → win funding → the grant pays your work. You don't enter "as a student"; you enter because **the project needs you**.

### 6. Industrial PhD ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
The Swiss PhD is a *job*, not the Italian model. Only worth it if: the topic coincides with the startup, the professor lets you build IP, and the lab collaborates with companies. Otherwise skip.

## The strategy shift that matters most
Old: "find a professor." New: **"find a professor who just won a grant."**

A professor without funds may love your research but *cannot hire you*. A professor with a
freshly funded grant often *needs* people to build, code, and do research. This is why the next
work item is a **funding due diligence** of the 15 groups — see `Group_Funding_DD.md`.

---

## Intuition
This profile fits **Research Engineer** better than **PhD Student**. Successful researchers
follow non-linear paths (Research Engineer → PhD → startup; Industry → academic collaboration →
spin-off; Postdoc → startup). There is no single road.
